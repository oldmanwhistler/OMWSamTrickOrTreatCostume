## What You Get

- Mask with same stats as cowboy hat. Needs 25 leather.
- Costume with same stats as duster. Needs 80 cloth.
- Flak Mask with same stats as flak helmet. Stuffable/Styleable.
- Flak Costume with same stats as flak jacket. Stuffable/Styleable.
- Fits children and all adult body sizes.

## Inspiration

Inspired by [Sam: The Soul of Halloween](https://samthesoulofhalloween.com/) created and owned by filmmaker Michael Dougherty. Sam was created in 1996 and can be seen in the 2007 movie "Trick R Treat" as well as several short films on YouTube and his website.

The costume was based off of the style of [Malang Bodies](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2838181007) by Plaxen but will fit any standard body replacer or vanilla bodies.
